Data-entry efficiency — proposed patterns

North star: minimize keystrokes per recording day (days are the high-frequency unit and ~90% repeat day-to-day). Each pattern pushes work up this ladder — turning typing into confirming:

1 · Inherit (animal/dataset)→ 2 · Carry forward (last day)→ 3 · Smart-default (common case)→ 4 · Quick-enter the deltas
Already good — not re-built: bad-channel marking is already a compact checkbox grid; cameras & recording-system are already catalog + per-day reference; type-+-Enter quick-add exists for simple lists (keywords, experimenters); keyboard shortcuts exist (Ctrl+S, Alt+←/→, Alt+N add-row, ?). These patterns are the model we extend below.
